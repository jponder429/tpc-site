The human layer has a team.

The Present Company brings AI training, governance, law, privacy and health expertise to the people side of AI risk. Here's who's behind it.

Jeanette Ponder, Chicago.

Jeanette Ponder

Founder and CEO

Jeanette is a Black woman founder who spent six years training frontier AI models, where she saw the gap firsthand: the rules on paper rarely match what people do with AI at work. She founded The Present Company in 2025 to close that gap with plain-language credentials and a record organizations can show.

Her background in politics, sociology and social justice shapes who TPC builds for, and 23 years in live production shaped how it builds: on deadline, in public, with no room for a second take.

  • Six years as an award-winning specialist-level trainer on Fortune 100 frontier AI platforms.
  • Microsoft Innovation Challenge Phase 1 awardee. Scouted by Atlassian, Dell and Salesforce.
  • 23 years in live production, from New York Fashion Week to Cirque du Soleil.
  • Admitted to Simon’s Rock College of Bard at 15. Studied voice at Sarah Lawrence College.
