Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026. This page applies to both thepresentco.com and thisisponder.com.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jeanette Ponder, doing business as The Present Company ("The Present Company," "TPC," "we," "us" or "our") collects through our websites, including thepresentco.com and thisisponder.com, our product demos and our forms (together, the "Sites"), and how we use and protect it. If you have a question, email us at info@thepresentco.com.

The short version

  • We collect what you choose to give us in our forms, such as your name, email and the details you share about your work.
  • We use it to reply to you, send the updates you asked for and improve our products.
  • We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising or use it to train AI models.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Information you give us

When you fill out a form on the Sites, we collect the information you enter. Depending on the form, that can include:

  • Waitlist and newsletter signups: your name, email address, industry, whether you are signing up for yourself or a team, and your consent to receive email from us.
  • Partner pages, such as our Women's Alliance of Financial Advisors page: your name, email, firm or practice name, role, specialty, what is motivating your interest and whether your firm may want a group license.
  • Present Practical requests: your name, work email, organization, role, team size and any details you choose to share about your organization's use of AI.
  • Investor inquiries: your name, email, firm and any message you send.
  • Emails and calls: anything you send us directly, and the details you enter if you book a call with us through Calendly.

Please do not send us confidential client, patient or company information through our forms.

Information collected automatically

  • Demo progress: our product demos save your progress, quiz answers and written reflections in your own browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. That information stays on your device unless a feature clearly tells you it will be sent to us. You can clear it at any time from the demo or your browser settings.
  • Quiz and survey results: some demo features may send us your answers and scores, along with a random identifier that is not tied to your name or email, so we can measure how well our lessons work. We report these results only in aggregate.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of your visit. We use it to keep the Sites running and secure.
  • Cookies: we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We store a small setting in your browser to remember choices such as whether you have already seen our waitlist pop-up. If we add analytics, we will use privacy-focused tools and update this policy.

How we use information

  • To respond to your requests, schedule calls and provide the products and services you ask for.
  • To send the emails you signed up for, such as waitlist news, launch updates and our newsletter. Every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • To understand interest in our products by industry and audience, and to improve our lessons, demos and Sites.
  • To keep the Sites secure, prevent spam and abuse, and comply with the law.

We do not use your personal information to train artificial intelligence models.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Sites, under terms that limit their use of it, and only as needed:

  • Vercel hosts the Sites.
  • Notion stores form submissions and our waitlist.
  • Resend sends our confirmation and update emails.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces on the Sites, which means your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google.
  • Calendly handles call scheduling if you choose to book a call.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights, safety or property of TPC or others, or as part of a merger, financing or sale of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, such as while you remain on a waitlist or mailing list, and then delete it or keep it only in a form that no longer identifies you. If you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any message. You can also email info@thepresentco.com to ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. Depending on where you live, including California and other U.S. states, the European Union and the United Kingdom, you may have additional rights under local law. We will respond within the time the law requires, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Children and students

The Sites are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and our forms are not designed for anyone under 18. Our student program, Ponder GenEthyx, is offered through schools and families: GenEthyx Home signups come from parents or guardians, and any student information we receive in the future through a school will be handled under our agreement with that school and the laws that apply, including FERPA and COPPA. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections to the Sites. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International visitors

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. By using the Sites, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products and the law change. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and if a change is significant, we will give you notice, for example by email or on the Sites.
