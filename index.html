Ponder5Q

One AI task, five questions, more clarity.

It's habitual: the Ponder 5Q ethical framework cuts through the usual academia-speak with an intuitive, easy-to-implement process made to curb indecision and negative impact.

Question 01 / 05
Who does this help?
Identify the intended and unintended beneficiaries.
Question 02 / 05
Who does this hurt?
Name the people and communities absorbing the cost.
Question 03 / 05
How has it already caused harm?
Reckon with the record honestly.
Question 04 / 05
How can we reasonably mitigate that harm?
Build the intervention into the process, not the postmortem.
Question 05 / 05
How do we prevent it from happening again?
Close the loop. Document it. Train to it.
